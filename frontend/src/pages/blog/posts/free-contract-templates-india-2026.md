## What makes a contract valid in India?

Under the [Indian Contract Act, 1872](/blog/indian-contract-act-essential-clauses), a contract is valid when there is:

1. **Offer and acceptance** — one party proposes, the other accepts without modification.
2. **Lawful consideration** — something of value flows both ways (money, services, goods).
3. **Capacity** — both parties are adults of sound mind, not disqualified by law.
4. **Free consent** — no coercion, fraud, misrepresentation or undue influence.
5. **Lawful object** — the purpose of the agreement is legal.

A contract does not need to be on stamp paper to be *valid*, but many agreements need proper **stamp duty** to be *admissible as evidence* in court. Rates vary by state — check our [stamp duty guide](/blog/stamp-duty-guide-india) or use the [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator).

## Which template do you actually need?

**Hiring someone?** If they work fixed hours under your control, use the [employment agreement](/template/employment); if they deliver outcomes on their own schedule, use the [freelancer agreement](/template/freelancer) — misclassifying an employee as a contractor can create PF, ESI and tax liabilities. For students and trainees, the [internship agreement](/template/internship-agreement) keeps the engagement clearly outside employment law while protecting your IP.

**Sharing sensitive information?** Sign an [NDA](/template/nda) *before* the first detailed conversation. See our guide on [how to write an NDA](/blog/how-to-write-an-nda) for what each clause does.

**Renting property?** Most residential rentals use an 11-month [leave and licence agreement](/template/rental). Our [rent agreement guide](/blog/rent-agreement-format-india) explains why 11 months and what registration costs.

**Starting a business with someone?** A [partnership deed](/blog/partnership-deed-format-india) decides profit sharing, capital, and what happens when a partner exits — decide it on paper while everyone is still friends.

**Lending money to a friend or relative?** A simple [loan agreement](/template/loan-agreement) with the amount, interest and repayment date converts an awkward conversation into a clean, enforceable record. Always transfer through bank channels, never cash.

### Are these templates legally valid in India?

The formats follow standard Indian drafting practice and reference Indian law (Indian Contract Act 1872, Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996). Validity of your final contract depends on correct execution — proper stamp duty, signatures, and for some documents registration — and on the specifics of your deal. For high-value agreements, have a lawyer review the draft.

### Do I need stamp paper for every contract?

No. A contract signed on plain paper is still a valid contract. But many states require stamp duty for specific instruments (rent agreements, loan agreements, partnership deeds) for the document to be admissible as evidence. Check your state's rates with the stamp duty calculator.

### Can I edit a template after downloading it?

### What is the difference between a contract and an agreement?

Every contract is an agreement, but not every agreement is a contract. An agreement becomes a contract only when it is enforceable by law — made with free consent, lawful consideration and a lawful object, by parties competent to contract (Section 10, Indian Contract Act 1872).
