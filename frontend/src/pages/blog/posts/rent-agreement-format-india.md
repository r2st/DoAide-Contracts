Whether you're a landlord letting a flat in Pune or a tenant moving to Bengaluru for a new job, a written **rent agreement** protects both sides. It records the rent, deposit, notice period and house rules, and it's often needed for address proof, HRA claims, police verification and bank KYC. In Hindi it's commonly called a *kiraya anubandh* or *kirayanama*.

This guide explains the standard **rent agreement format in India**, why most agreements run for 11 months, which clauses you must include, and how stamp duty and registration work. When you're ready, generate one in minutes with the [free rental agreement template](/template/rental).

> This article is general information, not legal advice. Rent laws, stamp duty and registration rules vary by state, so check with your local sub-registrar or a lawyer for your situation.

## Lease vs leave and licence: what's the difference?

There are two common types of rental arrangement in India:

| | Lease agreement | Leave and licence agreement |
|---|---|---|
| What it gives the occupant | An interest in the property | Only a permission to use it |
| Typical duration | 1 year or more | Often 11 months |
| Tenant protection | Stronger under state rent control laws | Easier for the owner to recover possession |
| Registration | Compulsory if for a year or more (Registration Act, 1908) | Depends on the state; mandatory in Maharashtra regardless of term |

Most residential rentals in cities use a **leave and licence** format because it's simpler and keeps the owner's rights clear.

## Why are rent agreements made for 11 months?

Under Section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908, leases of immovable property for a year or more must be registered. An **11-month rent agreement** stays under that threshold in most states, so the parties can often skip formal registration and pay lower stamp duty.

Two important caveats:

- **Maharashtra** requires leave and licence agreements to be registered whatever their duration, and offers online e-registration.
- An unregistered agreement may have limited value as evidence in some disputes. Registration gives stronger protection, especially for longer stays or high rents.

## Essential clauses in a rent agreement

A good rent agreement format covers these points:

1. **Parties:** full names, addresses and ID details of the landlord (licensor) and tenant (licensee).
2. **Property description:** full address, flat number, floor, carpet area, and parking if included.
3. **Term:** start date, end date and renewal terms (e.g. renewal with a 5-10% increase by mutual consent).
4. **Rent and due date:** monthly amount, due date (e.g. by the 5th), payment method (bank transfer or UPI), and any late fee.
5. **Security deposit:** amount, refund timeline after vacating, and permitted deductions for damage or unpaid bills.
6. **Maintenance and utilities:** who pays society maintenance, electricity, water, gas and internet.
7. **Repairs:** minor repairs by the tenant, major structural repairs by the owner.
8. **Use of premises:** residential only, no subletting, rules on pets and alterations.
9. **Lock-in period:** a minimum period (e.g. 6 months) during which neither side can terminate without paying a penalty.
10. **Notice period:** commonly one month from either side.
11. **Inventory:** a list of furniture, fittings and appliances, with their condition.
12. **Owner's right of inspection** with reasonable notice.
13. **Dispute resolution and jurisdiction.**
14. **Signatures of both parties and two witnesses.**

## Stamp duty on rent agreements

Rent agreements must be executed on stamp paper (or e-stamp) of the value prescribed by your state's Stamp Act. The duty usually depends on the rent, the deposit and the term, and it varies widely, from a small fixed amount in some states to a percentage of total rent plus deposit in others.

- Buy e-stamp paper through authorised vendors or the SHCIL e-stamping portal where available.
- Pay duty *before* signing. Under-stamped documents may be inadmissible as evidence until the deficit and a penalty are paid.

## Registration: when and how

- **Lease of 12 months or more:** registration is compulsory in all states.
- **Maharashtra:** register every leave and licence agreement. Online e-registration with Aadhaar-based biometric verification is available.
- **Other states:** 11-month agreements are commonly left unregistered, but you can still register for stronger evidence.

Registration usually needs both parties (or their authorised representatives), two witnesses, ID and address proof, property documents and the stamped agreement.

## Other compliance points

- **Police verification:** many cities require landlords to submit tenant verification to the local police. It's often available online.
- **Rent receipts and HRA:** salaried tenants claiming HRA need rent receipts. Above certain annual rent thresholds, the landlord's PAN may be required for the employer.
- **TDS on high rent:** individual tenants paying rent above ₹50,000 per month may need to deduct TDS under Section 194-IB. Check the current rate and rules on the Income Tax portal.
- **Model Tenancy Act, 2021:** provides a framework (including a cap on security deposits for residential premises), but it applies only where a state has adopted it.

## Sample format (outline)

```
LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on [date] at [city] between:
[Owner name], residing at [address] ("Licensor")
AND
[Tenant name], residing at [address] ("Licensee")

1. Property: [Flat no., building, address]
2. Term: 11 months from [start date] to [end date]
3. Licence fee: ₹[amount] per month, payable by the [5th] of each month
4. Security deposit: ₹[amount], refundable within [30] days of vacating
5. Lock-in: [6] months; notice period: [1] month
6. Maintenance & utilities: [who pays]
7. Use: residential only; no subletting
8. Inventory: as per Annexure A

Signed: Licensor ____  Licensee ____
Witness 1 ____  Witness 2 ____
```

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Signing on plain paper or under-stamped paper.
- Not recording the deposit refund timeline or permitted deductions.
- Leaving out the inventory list, which leads to disputes when the tenant vacates.
- No lock-in or notice clause.
- Cash payments without receipts.
- Not registering in states where registration is mandatory.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is an 11-month rent agreement legally valid?

Yes. An 11-month leave and licence agreement on the correct stamp paper and signed by both parties and witnesses is a valid contract. In most states it does not need compulsory registration, but in Maharashtra registration is mandatory for leave and licence agreements of any duration.

### Is it compulsory to register a rent agreement?

Registration is compulsory under the Registration Act, 1908, for leases of one year or more. For shorter agreements it depends on the state; Maharashtra, for example, requires all leave and licence agreements to be registered.

### How much stamp duty is payable on a rent agreement?

It depends on your state's Stamp Act and usually on the rent, the deposit and the term. Some states charge a small fixed amount for short agreements, while others charge a percentage. Use a stamp duty calculator and confirm with your state's official rates.

### Can I make a rent agreement online?

You can draft the agreement online and buy e-stamp paper online in many states. Some states, such as Maharashtra, also allow online registration of leave and licence agreements with biometric verification.

### What is the difference between a rent agreement and a lease deed?

A rent or leave and licence agreement usually gives the tenant only a permission to occupy, often for 11 months. A lease deed transfers an interest in the property for a longer term and must be registered if it runs for a year or more.
